-- this is for the cozy fall game jam (I am english, I like autumn more!)

# idea
a cozy farming simulator based around farming crops and getting upgrades / seeds to grow the farm
you sell to customers.
the customers want to buy vegetables based on your rep. 
each vegetable has some star ratings e.g. quality, size, ...
the star rating determines how satisfied the customer is, higher start mean a tip (💀 I know) and better rep, 
better rep results in richer customers coming who want to buy more!

#design
cosy autumnal themed farm, warm colours, vegetables, assets from kenney.nl
stardew valley (?) reference imagery
imagery is provided in skewmorphic styles, tooltips
e.g. plant info is on a card on a stick. seed info on a label on the sack, tool info similar


#upgrade path / inital start
you start with some farmland, and a few basic seeds.
you plant the seeds, they grow slowly, you give to customer: get money
there is a shop complete with awnings and shelves
you can browse through the shop and more will be revealed as you progress

there is a scarecrow that will act as the tutorial

we should provide hover effects and tooltips

sound is important: planting, buying, selling, growing (quietly in background), leaves falling slowly

slow animations to make it feel cosy and slow
